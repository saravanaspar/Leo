# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Leo welcomes contributors and users regardless of experience level or background. Project spaces should remain technically rigorous, respectful, and useful.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and code without attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- assuming good faith while still requiring evidence for technical claims;
- respecting different levels of experience;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- keeping disagreements focused on implementation, data, experiments, and documented project goals.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted insults;
- sexualized or degrading comments;
- deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- using project channels to spam, scam, or distribute malicious content;
- retaliating against someone for reporting a concern in good faith.

## Technical disagreements

Leo includes correctness-sensitive training and CUDA code. Strong disagreement is allowed; personal hostility is not. When claims conflict, prefer reproducible tests, profiler output, code references, and documented semantics over status or volume.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments/issues/PRs and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

If a conduct problem also involves a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and avoid exposing exploit details publicly.

## Scope

This policy applies to official Leo repository spaces and to public interactions in which someone is clearly representing the project.
